Calculation

Corrected Na = measured Na + correction factor × (glucose − 100) / 100

The units are mEq/L for sodium and mg/dL for glucose. Classification uses the Katz estimate and conventional adult sodium limits (135–145 mEq/L), not a treatment threshold.

Clinical interpretation

This is a mathematical estimate, not a validated treatment protocol. The two factors may diverge substantially at marked hyperglycemia; Hillier and colleagues reported a nonlinear relationship, particularly above 400 mg/dL. Interpret with measured electrolytes, tonicity, volume status, and serial results. Do not make treatment decisions using this value alone.
